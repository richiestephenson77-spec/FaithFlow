 SOURCES & INTERPRETATION

A map to explore.
Not certainty we don’t have.

This edition ends at AD 1600. Later scenes follow the history of biblical places; biblical people do not remain on the map after their selected periods.

People, groups and events

Original costume illustrations are illustrative, not historical portraits. Coordinates identify a sourced place associated with the narrative, not a person’s exact position. Popups distinguish place confidence, scriptural association and uncertain dates. Small display offsets keep markers readable; anchor lines point back to the source coordinate.

The patriarchal and Exodus chronology is conventional and disputed. Event windows may represent dating uncertainty rather than continuous activity. In-between dates with no supported episode show no figure. Journeys use sourced stops only; no road or sea path is invented.

What is sourced

  • OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data — CC BY 4.0, not public domain. Candidate locations and the source’s score fields are preserved.
  • Pleiades — selected actual records, CC BY 3.0. Representative points and source attestations are retained.
  • Natural Earth — public-domain SRTM-derived shaded relief, land, rivers and lakes. Modern physical reference, not ancient coastline reconstruction.
  • Bible Mapper Atlas — linked references only. Its map images are not bundled; published terms require permission for commercial use.

What is unavailable

No suitably licensed, period-specific political boundaries were sourced for this package. The optional territory layer uses newly authored schematic artwork, explicitly labeled illustrative. It is not sourced historical boundary data, and does not represent complete imperial extents. Intermediate animated shapes are visual effects, not dated reconstructions. Gazetteer points are never converted into invented kingdom borders. No Rose Publishing or commercial-atlas map artwork is included.

How to read confidence

OpenBible’s identification score is its own measure of support for a candidate place. It is not a measured probability, a date-confidence score or proof of the story’s historical occurrence. Null scores remain unavailable. Search and the optional undated gazetteer layer do not establish settlement lifespans.
